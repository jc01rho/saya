composition: Canvas: two viewports side by side at the same pixel scale, top edges aligned, on a flat warm-white backdrop, each frame outlined by a one-pixel slate line: the desktop frame 1440x900 at left, the mobile frame 390x844 at right. There is NO horizontal dividing line anywhere and no second horizontal band: each pane is one unbroken flat field from its top edge to its bottom edge. Desktop: the room list occupies the left quarter of the width as one limestone-cream field running the full height of the frame; the open conversation occupies the remaining three quarters as one pale sky-blue field running the full height; a single vertical one-pixel warm-white keyline divides them from the top edge to the bottom edge, interrupted for the height of the active room row 개발 잡담, leaving a visible gap that opens the list into the conversation. The list has no title and no search field; rows begin after a top margin of one row height; each row is the title in ink at the left with the relative time in the second tone at the right end of the same line, then the preview in the second tone on a second line with the unread numeral in ink at its right end; rows are separated by generous space rather than lines; the active row title is set in the heavier weight. Conversation: the room title 개발 잡담 in ink at the top; below it the coverage line in the second tone with a one-pixel line beneath it; then one single left-aligned message column at a measure of about 60% of the pane width, running downward in time order. Type character: a quiet humanist Korean sans such as Pretendard, one weight for all bodies at a comfortable reading size with generous line height, the heavier weight used only for the active room title and the mention token @사용자, tabular figures for times; small Hangul stays crisp and fully legible. Detail level is low: one door inset line, one shadow, one tiny cat, and otherwise only text and one-pixel lines; no icons except the mobile back chevron, no badges, no avatars, no emoji, no stickers, no checkmarks or read ticks. Emotional priorities in order: truthfulness of state, comfort reading long Korean text, calm and distinctiveness, speed of reply, feature breadth last. Uncertainty is an ordinary resting state carried by one quiet word at the smallest size, not an alert: nothing red, nothing green, no exclamation marks, no warning triangles, no spinners; the unreadable room and the pending message look as settled as everything else. Not a KakaoTalk skin, not a dashboard.
